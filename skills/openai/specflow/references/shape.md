# Idea shaping

Use Shape to clarify the problem, users, desired result, boundaries, constraints,
vocabulary, and success measures before discussing architecture.

Start with the user's words and distinguish their statements from inference.
With `auto`, choose recommendations or reversible assumptions under
[Modifiers](modifiers.md). Otherwise ask only questions that change scope or
unlock useful detail. Use
[Probe](probe.md) for consequential uncertainty.

A short brief is optional. Use the project's location or one suited to the
request; no prescribed intent file is required. If saving assumptions or other
records, follow [Artifact model](artifact-model.md).

Shape is sufficient when the next work can proceed without inventing product
policy. Leave remaining questions visible where they still block that work.
